About

I'm a photographer and filmmaker based in Bangkok. I shoot and edit interviews, vox pops, premiere coverage and behind-the-scenes films for movie releases in Thailand, along with original short films and music videos.

Most of the job happens off camera: getting a nervous voice actor to relax, finding a quiet corner at a red carpet, cutting a reaction video the same night. That's why the process gets its own page here.

Worked with: HAAB, Disney+ Thailand, UIP Thailand
